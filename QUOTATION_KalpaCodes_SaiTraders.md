## Project: WhatsApp AI Saree Intelligence Bot

Complete AI-powered WhatsApp commerce solution with Google Gemini AI visual search, Tally ERP integration, and Admin Web Dashboard for managing your saree business end-to-end.

---

## 1. Features Included

### A. WhatsApp AI Automation

| # | Feature | Description |
|:--|:--------|:------------|
| 1 | AI Visual Saree Search | Customers send a photo of any saree → Gemini AI identifies fabric, weave style, color, pattern, border, pallu, occasion & estimated market price |
| 2 | Smart Catalog Matching | AI-powered 768-dimensional vector similarity search finds the closest matching sarees from your inventory |
| 3 | Interactive Product Cards | Rich WhatsApp cards with saree images, prices, MRP, stock status, and tap-to-action buttons |
| 4 | Natural Language Search | Customers type queries like _"red silk sarees under ₹8000"_ and get instant AI-matched results |
| 5 | Conversational Ordering | Full order flow on WhatsApp — select saree → provide address → receive confirmed order number (SAI-XXXX) |
| 6 | Order Tracking | Customers check order status anytime by typing _"Track SAI-1001"_ |
| 7 | Smart Intent Detection | AI understands greetings, product searches, confirmations, address inputs, and order queries automatically |
| 8 | Automated Welcome Messages | Personalized Namaste greeting with quick action buttons for every customer |
| 9 | Blue Tick Read Receipts | Automatic read receipt marking for professional customer experience |
| 10 | Message Deduplication | Prevents duplicate processing from Meta webhook retries |
| 11 | Customer Profile Management | Auto-created records with phone number, name, saved address, and full conversation history |
| 12 | Stateful Conversations | Smart conversation state machine (idle → analyzing → results → ordering → confirmed) |

### B. Tally ERP Integration

| # | Feature | Description |
|:--|:--------|:------------|
| 1 | Auto Invoice Sync | WhatsApp orders automatically pushed to Tally as Sales Vouchers |
| 2 | Stock Sync | Real-time inventory sync between Tally and WhatsApp bot catalog |
| 3 | Customer Ledger Sync | WhatsApp customers auto-synced as Tally Ledger entries (Sundry Debtors) |
| 4 | Payment Status Mapping | COD / UPI payment statuses reflected in Tally receipt entries |
| 5 | GST-Ready Invoicing | Tax-compliant invoice generation with HSN codes for textile/saree category |

### C. Admin Web Dashboard

| # | Feature | Description |
|:--|:--------|:------------|
| 1 | Saree Catalog Manager | Add, edit, delete sarees with images, pricing, and auto-generated AI embeddings |
| 2 | Order Management | View all orders, update status (Pending → Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Delivered) |
| 3 | Customer Directory | Browse all WhatsApp customers with conversation history and order count |
| 4 | Image Analysis Log | View every saree photo analyzed by AI with extracted attributes and match results |
| 5 | Conversation Monitor | Real-time view of active WhatsApp conversations and their states |
| 6 | Analytics Dashboard | Daily / weekly / monthly stats — orders, revenue, popular searches, top products |
| 7 | Image Upload | Drag-and-drop saree image upload to cloud storage with auto-optimization |
| 8 | Settings & Configuration | Configure welcome messages, similarity thresholds, max recommendations, business profile |

### D. AI & Intelligence Engine

| # | Feature | Description |
|:--|:--------|:------------|
| 1 | Google Gemini Vision AI | State-of-the-art multimodal AI for saree photo analysis and attribute extraction |
| 2 | Vector Embedding Search | 768-dimensional semantic search using pgvector for intelligent saree matching |
| 3 | AI Intent Classification | Gemini-powered customer intent understanding with smart regex fallback |
| 4 | Model Auto-Fallback | Automatic fallback across multiple Gemini model versions if primary is unavailable |

## 3. Terms & Conditions

### 3.1 Payment Terms

1. **Setup Fee:** ₹10,000 (one-time), payable 100% in advance before project kickoff.
2. **Monthly Fee:** ₹5,000, payable by the 5th of each calendar month, in advance.
3. **Third-Party Costs:** Google Gemini and Meta WhatsApp charges are billed at actuals and invoiced monthly in arrears, payable within 7 days of invoice date.
4. **Overage Charges:** User count beyond 1,000 is calculated at month-end and invoiced along with the next monthly cycle.
5. **Payment Mode:** Bank Transfer (NEFT / IMPS / UPI) or as mutually agreed.
6. **Late Payment:** Overdue invoices attract a late fee of 1.5% per month from the due date.

### 3.2 Third-Party Price Adjustment Clause

7. The Google Gemini AI and Meta WhatsApp Business API rates quoted in this document are based on their officially published pricing as of **September 29, 2026**. These rates are determined solely by Google and Meta respectively and are outside the control of KalpaCodes.
8. **If Google or Meta increases their API pricing**, the revised rates shall be passed through to the Client at actuals, effective from the date of the price change by the respective platform.
9. KalpaCodes shall notify the Client in writing (via email or WhatsApp) at least **7 days before** applying any third-party price increase to the Client's invoice.
10. **If Google or Meta decreases their API pricing**, the reduced rates shall similarly be passed through to the Client, effective immediately.
11. The Client may request supporting documentation (API billing dashboard screenshots or invoices) from KalpaCodes to verify third-party charges at any time.

### 3.3 Service Scope

12. The monthly service fee covers: hosting, maintenance, monitoring, bug fixes, security patches, and up to **2 hours of support or minor modifications** per month.
13. Feature enhancements, new integrations, or major modifications beyond the included scope shall be quoted separately at KalpaCodes' standard development rates.
14. Tally integration assumes **Tally Prime** or **Tally ERP 9** with XML / ODBC gateway enabled on the Client's infrastructure.

### 3.4 User Limit & Scaling

15. The base plan includes up to **1,000 unique WhatsApp users per calendar month**.
16. A "user" is defined as a unique phone number that initiates at least one inbound message to the WhatsApp bot during a calendar month.
17. Users beyond 1,000 are charged at **₹5 per additional user per month**, calculated and invoiced at the end of each calendar month.
18. KalpaCodes shall provide a monthly user count report along with each invoice for transparency.

### 3.5 Uptime & Support

19. KalpaCodes targets **99.5% uptime** for all hosted services (edge functions, database, admin dashboard).
20. Third-party uptime (WhatsApp Cloud API, Google Gemini API, Supabase) is subject to the respective providers' SLAs and is not guaranteed by KalpaCodes.
21. Support is available **Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST**, via WhatsApp, email, or phone.
22. Critical issue response time: within **4 business hours**.

### 3.6 Data Ownership & Privacy

23. All customer data (phone numbers, addresses, orders, conversation history) is stored in the Client's own cloud database instance.
24. KalpaCodes shall not share, sell, or use Client or end-customer data for any purpose other than operating and maintaining this service.
25. The Client is responsible for complying with applicable data protection laws and Meta's WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.

27. After the minimum period, either party may terminate the agreement with **30 days' written notice**.
28. Upon termination, KalpaCodes shall provide a complete data export (database backup, media assets, source code handover) within 7 working days.
29. The one-time setup fee is **non-refundable**.
